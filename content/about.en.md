## Rodrigo Medeiros

Software engineer. Backend, frontend, infrastructure, and security: I work across the whole stack and reach for many languages and paradigms, picking the tool for the problem, not for the hype. I have a soft spot for functional programming and languages like Rust, the kind that force me to think before I write.

Day to day I build and operate production systems — APIs and services, deploy and release automation, and infrastructure across the major clouds (AWS, Azure, and GCP) — going down a layer when I need to, from the code to the kernel. Security rides along: I think about systems from the attacker's side too.

The homelab is where I take all of that to the extreme for the fun of it: virtualization, segmented networks, and a self-hosted AI stack running at home. Most of what I write here comes from there.

This blog is my public documentation: a place where I record what I learn, build, and explore. I write first and foremost for my future self, who will forget the details. If it helps someone else along the way, even better.
